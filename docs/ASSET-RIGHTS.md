# Asset Rights Status

Skyreach Isles is a public repository, so source provenance and redistribution rights are tracked separately from technical integration.

## Rights documented

- NASA SLS, the Blender-derived mobile SLS, Blue Marble, and NASA CGI Moon Kit LRO/LOLA assets have provenance recorded under `public/assets/space/`. The mobile SLS remains derived from the preserved original NASA geometry.
- NASA Moon imagery/elevation provenance and source URLs are recorded in `public/assets/space/moon-provenance.json`.
- The curated environment/animal pass uses Quaternius assets recorded as CC0 in the environment manifest and bundled license text.
- Generated/procedural repository-authored visuals do not depend on a third-party model license.

## User-provided assets requiring source-license confirmation

The following files were supplied directly during development and are intentionally **not** assigned a third-party license by this repository:

- `public/assets/space/alien/alien_spaceship.glb`
- `public/assets/space/alien/alien_ship.glb`
- `public/assets/boats/speedboat.glb`
- `public/assets/aircraft/airliner.glb`
- `public/assets/world/lax_airport.glb`
- `public/assets/space/jwst.glb`
- `public/assets/characters/nightweaver_LOD0.glb`

Their provenance files record hashes and integration details. Public availability in this repository should not be interpreted as a claim that third-party redistribution or commercial-use rights have been independently verified.

Before distributing a commercial build or reusing these raw models outside this project, confirm their original source/license or replace them with assets whose redistribution terms are documented.
